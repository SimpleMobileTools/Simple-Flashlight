# Contributing

Peitsman Flashlight is an internal Peitsman fork of the open-source Simple Flashlight, intentionally kept minimal: a flashlight on/off button and a brightness slider.

### Reporting issues
Open an issue on this repository. Please include your device model, Android version, and clear steps to reproduce.

### Scope
The app is meant to stay simple. Pull requests that add features (stroboscope, SOS, widgets, settings, etc.) fall outside the goal of this fork and will generally not be merged. Bug fixes, build/dependency fixes, branding/house-style corrections, and translation improvements are welcome.

### Branding
Any visual change must follow the Peitsman Brand Identity Guidelines (v1.5). The brand colors are defined in [app/src/main/res/values/colors.xml](app/src/main/res/values/colors.xml): brand red `#FF3C00`, smoky black `#141312`, off-white `#F6F3F1`.

### Building
See the build instructions in the [README](README.md). Note: you need a full JDK 17 that includes the `jmods` directory.
