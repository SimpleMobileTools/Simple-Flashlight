Peitsman Flashlight is a deliberately simple flashlight app in the Peitsman house style.

There are no menus and no clutter. You get one big button that turns your device's LED on and off, and a slider to set the brightness (on devices that support it, Android 13+). That's it.

What it does NOT have, on purpose:
- No stroboscope or SOS mode
- No bright display
- No sleep timer
- No home-screen widget or quick-settings tile
- No settings or about screen

Privacy first:
- Requires only the flashlight permission and a device with a camera flash
- No internet access, no ads, no tracking, no account

The launcher icon follows the Peitsman Brand Identity Guidelines: the flashlight glyph on the Peitsman brand red.

Open source, released under the GPL-3.0 license. Based on the open-source Simple Flashlight by Simple Mobile Tools, simplified and rebranded for Peitsman.
